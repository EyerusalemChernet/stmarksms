PROBLEM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When clicking payroll table buttons, users got error:
  "Invalid payroll ID"

Even though payroll records existed.

ROOT CAUSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The ID validation was too strict (is_numeric() check).

SOLUTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ Changed ID validation from:
   - is_numeric() check (strict)
   To:
   - intval() conversion (flexible)

✅ Updated 10 PayrollController methods:
   - edit()
   - show()
   - pdf()
   - export()
   - update()
   - approve()
   - markPaid()
   - revertToDraft()
   - addItem()
   - removeItem()
