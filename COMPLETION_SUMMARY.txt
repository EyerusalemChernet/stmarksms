HOW TO USE THE NEW LAYOUT
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

EXPORT ENTIRE PAYROLL LIST
├─ Step 1: Go to HR → Payroll
├─ Step 2: Select filters (optional)
└─ Step 3: Click [Export PDF] or [Export CSV] at top right

VIEW INDIVIDUAL PAYROLL
├─ Step 1: Find employee row
└─ Step 2: Click [👁️ View] button

EDIT PAYROLL (Draft Only)
├─ Step 1: Find draft payroll row
└─ Step 2: Click [✏️ Edit] button

APPROVE PAYROLL
├─ Step 1: Find draft payroll row
└─ Step 2: Click [✓ Approve] button

MARK AS PAID
├─ Step 1: Find approved payroll row
└─ Step 2: Click [💰 Mark Paid] button
